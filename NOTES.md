## Basics

### Instrument

Whatever you are working with could be a stock like Apple, Nvidia or commodity like gold or silver, etc.

### Order Book

Keeps all the live orders for buy and sell

#### ASK

price you buy at (imagine this is all the prices people are asking for the instrument)  
**BEST ASK** is the cheapest somebody is selling at (so to you cheapest you can buy at)

#### BID

price you can sell at (imagine this is all the prices people are willing to buy the instrument for)
**BEST BID** is the highest somebody is buying at (so to you highest you can sell at)

#### Spread

Difference between the best ask minus best bid

#### Mid

Midpoint/average of the best bid and ask.

#### Tick Size

Smallest allowed price step

#### Lot

One unit of volume

#### Liquidity

How easily you can trade without moving the price

**NOTE** most exchanges match orders by best price first and then earliest time at that price (which order came in first)
